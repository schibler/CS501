# (TA) Teaching Journal

**Who:** TA's

**Submission:** Once at end of week 4, and once at end of week 10

**Estimated Time:** ~15 minutes per week

## What you need to to
Keep a teaching journal each week, that details:
1. How much time you spent on what teaching-related duties?
2. What did you do in section? How did it go? What worked? What could be improved?

Submit it to Thomas twice during the quarter:
- By **Sunday, October 25th**, submit your teaching journal to Thomas via email, complete for weeks 1-4. It can be in any (reasonable) format, e.g. pdf, plain text, picture of handwritten notebook, etc. Don't worry about polishing it (bullet points fine!); I'm not going to judge the quality of your ideas/prose. That being said, you're welcome to ask any questions as part of your submission, and I'll be happy to take a closer look.
- By the end of week 10, **Sunday, December 6th**, submit your teaching journal to Thomas via email, complete for weeks 5-10 (you can include weeks 1-4 if it's easier). I'll send a reminder out via email closer to this date.

## What I hope you get out of it
* A habit of reflecting on what you learn about teaching -- these reflections can go in your teaching philosophy statement
* Better time management, especially to ensure you aren't spending too much time TA'ing
